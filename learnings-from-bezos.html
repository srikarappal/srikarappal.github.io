Learnings from Bezos: Time at Amazon Research

I spent a decade at Amazon, most of it in research. I came to work on hard problems and stayed because the place kept handing me bigger ones. I got to build things that reached hundreds of millions of people, alongside colleagues far smarter than me, trusted to be wrong on the way to being right.

Two of the leadership principles, "Think Big" and "Bias for Action", are etched in my thought process now. Think Big nudges me to pick problems a size too large on purpose. Bias for Action reminds me that most decisions are reversible, that a rough version shipped beats a perfect plan on paper. I reach for both daily, at work and at home: aim higher than is comfortable, then start before you feel ready.
